Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: we use the details you send us to reply to your enquiry and to deliver our services. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing unless you ask for it. This notice explains the detail, as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

The responsible party for the personal information described here is TechEssentials (Pty) Ltd, based in Blue Hills, Midrand, Gauteng, South Africa.

  • Email: info@techessentials.co.za
  • Phone: +27 62 322 7297
  • Information Officer: the director of TechEssentials (Pty) Ltd, reachable at the details above

2. What information we collect

  • Through the contact form: your name, email address, and the message you write, including anything you choose to include in it.
  • When you email, phone or WhatsApp us: your contact details and the content of your message.
  • When you visit the website: our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so that the site can be delivered and kept secure.

Please do not send us sensitive information, such as identity numbers, health details or passwords, through the form or by email.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss how we can help.
  • To prepare proposals, quotes and, where you engage us, to deliver our services.
  • To keep records of our communications.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We will not use your details for marketing, and we will not share them for marketing purposes, unless you have asked us to or agreed to it.

4. Do you have to give us this information?

No, giving us your information is voluntary. However, we need at least a name and a way to reach you in order to respond. If you do not provide them, we will not be able to answer your enquiry.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use the following service providers to run our website and email. They handle information on our behalf and under their own terms and data protection commitments:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts and secures our website.
  • Resend, which delivers messages sent through the contact form to our mailbox.
  • Our email hosting provider, which stores the emails we send and receive.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that form submissions come from a real person and not an automated bot. It may process technical signals from your browser, such as your IP address, for that purpose.
  • WhatsApp, only if you choose to contact us there. WhatsApp handles your messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may also disclose information where the law requires us to.

6. Information that leaves South Africa

Some of our providers process or store information outside South Africa, including in Europe. We choose providers that publish their own data protection commitments, and we only send them what is needed to run the service.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails and form messages only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and keep a reasonable record of it, generally up to 90 days after our last contact with you. We keep information longer where a contract or the law requires it.

8. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information, including encrypted connections to this website and password-protected mailboxes. If there is a security compromise that affects your information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as the law requires.

9. Cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. Our hosting provider may use technical cookies or logs that are necessary to deliver and secure the site.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and ask for a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, outdated or no longer needed;
  • object to us using your information, or withdraw your consent where we rely on it;
  • complain to the Information Regulator if you believe we have not handled your information properly. The Regulator's website is inforegulator.org.za.

To use any of these rights, email us at info@techessentials.co.za and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
